Pricing: Free, Team, Business and Compliance

Prices are per product. Free is the full product. Paid tiers add things a team or a company needs, never the security patches.

TierPriceWhat you get
Free$0, MIT licenceThe full cache server, self-hosted. Public images including the -fips image. Every security patch, never withheld. Basic Auth with a read-write and a read-only login, /metrics, the status page, all docs, and all public release evidence. Support through GitHub issues, best effort.
Team$49 a month, or $490 a yearA licence key that adds multi-user access control, SSO (OIDC), per-project usage analytics, and email support with a one-business-day target.
Business$199 a month, or $1,990 a yearEverything in Team, plus HA and replication across nodes, and priority support with the 24 to 48 hour CVE-response target.
Compliance$499 a month, billed annually ($5,988 a year)Everything in Business, plus a FIPS applicability statement, per-release attestation letters signed by FosterStack LLC and addressed to you, security-questionnaire support up to 8 hours a year (async, with a 10-business-day turnaround), and named-version long-term support.

What stays free

Security patches are never withheld from the free tier. The security evidence underneath is public and free too: SBOMs, build provenance, signatures, vulnerability statements and the FIPS module certificate number. Anyone can check it with no account and no purchase.

What Compliance sells

Not access to files or evidence, which are free. It sells the written analysis that maps the validated crypto module onto this product, the letters signed by us, and our time on your questionnaires. FosterStack Cache is not "FedRAMP compliant" or "CMMC compliant". Those attach to your service and your organisation, never to a component you deploy. This is validated crypto and publishable evidence for your compliance program. See who this is for.

How billing works

Self-serve, by credit card, with no sales call and no per-seat charge. Team and Business can be cancelled any time. Compliance is an annual term. Existing customers keep their price if prices rise.

Support, in plain words

The CVE-response target is a target, not a contractual penalty clause. Long-term support means a small published set of supported release lines, never a frozen branch for one customer.
